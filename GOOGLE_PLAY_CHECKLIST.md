# Google Play checklist

- Nazwa: Tarnobrzeg 112.
- Pakiet: `pl.tarnobrzeg112.app`.
- Kategoria: Komunikacja.
- Ikona: `play_store_assets/app_icon.png`.
- Feature graphic: `play_store_assets/feature_graphic.png`.
- Screenshoty: `play_store_assets/screenshots/`.
- Opis krotki: `play_store_assets/short_description.txt`.
- Opis pelny: `play_store_assets/full_description.txt`.
- Release notes: `play_store_assets/release_notes_internal_testing.txt`.
- Data safety: `play_store_assets/data_safety_notes.txt`.
- Polityka prywatnosci: opublikuj publiczny URL na podstawie `privacy_policy_draft.txt`.
- Wgraj AAB z `build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab`.
- Zachowaj bezpiecznie upload key z `android/app/upload-keystore.jks`.
